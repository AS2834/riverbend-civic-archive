About the Archive

The archive began as a set of donated filing cabinets in the west room of city hall. Volunteers scanned public records, wrote object notes, and maintained this small mirror for patrons who could not visit during open hours.

Some private accession material is intentionally absent from the public navigation. The old web mirror sometimes still remembers where it was stored.

Access

Public minutes and program notes are available without appointment. Physical objects require a volunteer to open the cabinet.
