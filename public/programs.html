Programs

Saturday object tables are informal show-and-tell sessions for neighbors. Old signs, municipal tools, fair programs, and building fragments are displayed with handwritten labels.

  • First Saturday: river trade maps
  • Second Saturday: schoolhouse photographs
  • Third Saturday: renovation fragments
